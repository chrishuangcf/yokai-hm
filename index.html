One shrine road. Six spirits. One roll.

Clash of Yokai is a dice game on a lantern-lit road in Kyoto. Six dice, three rolls, and the temple courtyard pays a point to the spirit who holds it. Reach 20, or be the last one still on the stones.

Look through the night

Every picture on this page is a frame from the game. The match is turn 1 in Kyoto: Oni, with Kitsune, Tengu, and Kappa. The temple courtyard is still open.

Original opening

The shrine road renders while you watch.

There is no video file. The title over the gate, the spirit in the lantern street, and the courtyard full of dice are the same scene the match uses. Skip it whenever you want. When it ends, you summon the table. There is no second menu in between.

The six

Summon the table. The night fills the other seats.

After the opening, the shrine is already built. Choose Normal for dice and power cards, or Advanced when you want each spirit’s own ability. This night is one player and four characters. Oni sits first.

Setup

Rule the lantern-lit night.

The line is on the setup screen, over the gate. The crown reads 20. Four characters is the table in these pictures. Two people can pass the device, and the empty seats stay with the night.

The turn

Six dice on the stones. Three chances.

Roll, tap what you want to keep, and throw the rest. The temple courtyard is worth a point to the spirit inside the circle. The others begin toward the outer shrine. Energy, when a roll produces it, is spent in the power market before the turn is handed on.

01

Roll

Six dice, up to three times. A die you tap stays on the stones. The others are thrown again.

02

Courtyard

The temple courtyard pays one point to the spirit who enters it. The outer shrine is the rest of the road.

03

Powers

The power market opens after the roll. Twenty points ends the night. So does being the last spirit standing.
