Support

Need a hand with Clash of Yokai? Start with the answers below. If you are still stuck, we are happy to help.

Contact us

Email photodesignch@gmail.com and include your device (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air), your iOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot helps too. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Summon your spirits, then roll the six dice up to three times each turn. Attack, heal, score, and spend energy on power cards. The first spirit to twenty points wins the shrine road.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Two people can pass the device, and computer spirits take the empty seats. There is no online play.

How do I continue a match?

Choose Continue on the menu. It appears when a match is saved on this device.

Does it need the internet?

No. The game runs entirely on your device and works offline.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, resets it, and we are not able to recover it.

The game feels slow or looks wrong.

Close other apps, restart the game, and make sure your device is running the latest iOS. If it continues, email us with your device model.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad.
