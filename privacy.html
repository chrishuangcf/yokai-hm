Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Clash of Yokai is a dice game on a lantern-lit shrine road. This page explains what the game stores and what it does not.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to play.
  • The game does not collect personal information, and does not send your play data to us or anyone else.
  • The game does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

To keep your place between sessions, the game saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage: the shrine road, your spirits, and your saved match, and your settings. This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, or device identifiers. The game has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party analytics or advertising tools, and it runs without an internet connection. If you download it from the App Store, Apple handles the download under its own privacy policy. If you visit this website, your host may keep standard server logs such as your IP address and the pages you opened.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes what it collects, we will update this page and the date above before the change goes live.
